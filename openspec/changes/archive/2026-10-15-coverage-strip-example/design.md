# Design

## Context

- **The diagram needs one shot that covers the stay and one that expires during it.** Biscuit's card has neither failure, so his real dates can't illustrate the rule.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** nothing on the home page contradicts the vaccine card it shows.

**Non-Goals:** redrawing the diagram or changing which rule it illustrates.

## Decisions

### D1. A hypothetical dog, not a named pet
- **No name:** any name could match a seeded pet now or later and invite the same comparison. "A hypothetical dog" says plainly that the dates are illustrative.
- **Alternative considered:** using Biscuit's real expiry dates. Every shot covers his stay, so the failing case would disappear.
